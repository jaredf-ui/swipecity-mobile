# Privacy Policy for SwipeCity

**Last Updated: August 2025**

## Introduction

SwipeCity ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the SwipeCity mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Account Information**: Email address, display name, and profile photo
- **Profile Data**: Shopping preferences, categories of interest, and onboarding responses
- **User-Generated Content**: Items you list for sale, photos you upload, and messages you send

### Usage Information
- **App Activity**: Items you view, like, or save
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, and app version
- **Performance Data**: App crashes and usage statistics (anonymized)

### Location Information
- We do not currently collect location data, but may add this feature in future versions with your explicit consent

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide and improve the SwipeCity marketplace experience
- Enable you to create listings and communicate with other users
- Personalize your shopping and selling experience
- Send important updates about your account or the service
- Analyze app usage to improve performance and features
- Prevent fraud and ensure platform safety

## Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in these limited circumstances:
- **With other users**: Your public profile information and listings are visible to other SwipeCity users
- **Service providers**: We use Firebase (Google) for authentication and data storage
- **Legal requirements**: If required by law or to protect our rights and safety

## Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:
- Secure authentication through Firebase
- Encrypted data transmission
- Regular security updates and monitoring
- Limited access to personal information by our team

## Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:
- Access and update your account information
- Delete your account and associated data
- Control what information you share in your profile
- Opt out of non-essential communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@swipecity.app

## Children's Privacy

SwipeCity is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may delete your account at any time through the app settings.

## Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase for:
- User authentication
- Data storage and synchronization
- App analytics and crash reporting

Firebase's privacy practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
- Email: privacy@swipecity.app
- Support: help@swipecity.app
