# SwipeCity Marketplace - Support

## 🆘 Getting Help

If you're experiencing issues with SwipeCity Marketplace, we're here to help!

### Common Issues & Solutions

#### Can't Log In
- Make sure you're using the correct email and password
- Check your internet connection
- Try resetting your password through the app
- Clear the app data and try again

#### App Crashes or Freezes
- Force close the app and reopen it
- Check for app updates in the App Store
- Restart your device
- Ensure you have enough storage space on your device

#### Messages Not Sending
- Check your internet connection
- Make sure you're logged in
- Try refreshing the chat by pulling down
- Close and reopen the chat

#### Photos Not Loading
- Check your internet connection
- Allow the app to access photos in your device settings
- Try uploading smaller image files
- Ensure the image format is supported (JPG, PNG)

### Contact Support

For issues not covered above, please reach out through one of these channels:

1. **GitHub Issues** (Recommended)
   - Visit: https://github.com/jaredf-ui/swipecity-mobile/issues
   - Click "New Issue"
   - Describe your problem with as much detail as possible
   - Include your device model and iOS version

2. **Email Support**
   - Email: jaredfeeny@gmail.com
   - Subject: SwipeCity Support - [Brief Description]
   - Include:
     - Device model (e.g., iPhone 14 Pro)
     - iOS version
     - App version
     - Description of the issue
     - Screenshots if applicable

### Bug Reports

When reporting a bug, please include:
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened
- Screenshots or screen recordings if possible
- Your device information
