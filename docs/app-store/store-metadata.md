### Full Description
SwipeCity transforms local marketplace shopping into an engaging, swipe-based experience. Browse through curated items from sellers in your area, save favorites, and connect directly with sellers.

**Features:**
• 📱 Intuitive swipe interface for browsing items
• 💰 Wide variety of categories: Electronics, Fashion, Home & Garden, and more
• 💬 Direct messaging with sellers
• ❤️ Save and organize your favorite items
• 🔍 Smart filtering and search
• 📸 High-quality product photos
• 🛡️ Safe and secure transactions

Whether you're hunting for vintage treasures, tech gadgets, or everyday essentials, SwipeCity makes discovering local marketplace gems fun and effortless.

**Perfect for:**
- Bargain hunters and treasure seekers
- Students looking for affordable items
- Anyone who loves discovering unique finds
- Sellers wanting to reach more buyers

Join the SwipeCity community and start swiping your way to great deals today!
