## App Description (4000 characters max)

SwipeCity revolutionizes local marketplace shopping with an intuitive swipe interface inspired by dating apps. Discover items from your community, connect with sellers, and make deals - all with a simple swipe!

**Key Features:**

🛍️ **Swipe to Shop**
Browse local marketplace items with an engaging swipe interface. Swipe right on items you love, left to pass. It's shopping made fun and effortless!

💬 **Instant Messaging**
Match with sellers instantly and chat directly within the app. Negotiate prices, ask questions, and arrange pickups seamlessly.

🏪 **Seller Mode**
Switch to seller mode to list your items in minutes. Upload photos, set prices, and reach buyers in your area instantly.

📍 **Location-Based Discovery**
Find items near you with smart location filtering. Set your preferred radius and discover hidden gems in your neighborhood.

🔍 **Smart Categories**
Browse through organized categories including Electronics, Fashion, Home & Garden, Sports, Books, and more. Find exactly what you're looking for or discover something new!

💰 **Price Filtering**
Set your budget and only see items within your price range. No more scrolling through items you can't afford.

⭐ **Save Favorites**
Build your wishlist by saving items you're interested in. Come back to them anytime when you're ready to buy.

📊 **Seller Dashboard**
Track your listings, manage conversations, and monitor your sales all from one convenient dashboard.

**Why Choose SwipeCity?**

- **Fast & Intuitive**: Our swipe interface makes browsing hundreds of items quick and enjoyable
- **Safe & Secure**: All communications happen within the app with Firebase-powered security
- **Community Focused**: Support local sellers and reduce waste by buying pre-loved items
- **Real-Time Updates**: Get instant notifications when sellers respond or new items match your interests
- **Cross-Platform**: Seamlessly sync your data across all your devices

Whether you're decluttering your home, hunting for unique finds, or building a side business, SwipeCity makes local buying and selling more enjoyable than ever before.

Join thousands of users who have already discovered the joy of swipe shopping. Download SwipeCity today and transform the way you shop locally!
